Hello! I’m Abd Alrahman, a passionate and curious person who loves exploring new ideas and solving problems. I have a strong interest in programming and cybersecurity, and I aim to build a bright future in this field.
I also enjoy mental challenges, like solving Rubik’s Cubes, and I’m interested in sports to stay active and energized. I believe in creativity, dedication, and teamwork, and I always strive to bring positivity to everything I do.
Let’s connect and achieve great things together!

About Me

I was born in Tripoli, Libya, in 2008. I am a Libyan Muslim of Amazigh origin from the city of Al-Qalaa. I studied at Al-Zahraa Primary School and excelled academically. I was a respectful and high-achieving student and ranked third in the municipality in the preparatory certificate exams. Since my childhood, I enjoyed hobbies like playing football and later joined the athletics team for people with disabilities.

Programming & Cybersecurity

I developed a passion for programming in the ninth grade, learning independently. I started with WordPress, then explored graphic design, and later learned the basics of Python. I took a web development course with Engineer Silwan Ismail using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap, which significantly improved my skills. Later, I continued learning through YouTube tutorials, and took courses in JavaScript, Laravel, and Django. I worked on various projects such as an archiving system, a debts management system, an online lessons platform, and delivery systems, participating in real-world job opportunities.

Sports

I began with football, then switched to table tennis for about six months, and later entered athletics, starting with discus and shot put. Upon joining the national team, I focused on running and long jump, participating in international competitions in Morocco and Tunisia, and an Arab championship in Algeria, ranking third, while locally achieving first place with a time of 13.00 seconds in the 100 meters. I later retired from sports to focus on my studies.

Rubik’s Cube

I learned to solve the Rubik’s Cube via YouTube since the seventh grade. Despite having only one hand, I persisted and improved my skills. I participated in a Ramadan championship in Tripoli, ranking third in an open tournament, with a 30-second time for a 3x3 cube. Later, I began training children in this game through lessons and books.
